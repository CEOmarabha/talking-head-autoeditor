# Using AutoEditor (for friends) — plain and simple

You set this up once. After that it's just a website. Don't worry if you've
never done anything technical; every step is spelled out.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
## PART 1 — One-time setup (about 10 minutes, you only do this once)

Your own computer is what turns your footage into the finished video, so
there's a small "Helper" to set up first. To set it up you'll paste a few
lines into your computer's built-in command app. Here's exactly how.

### Step A — Open the command app on your computer

**If you have a Windows PC:**
1. Look at the very bottom-left of your screen for the search box (it says
   "Type here to search," next to the Windows logo). Click it.
2. Type the word:  `powershell`
3. In the results, click **Windows PowerShell** (blue icon). A dark window
   opens. That's the app you paste into.

**If you have a Mac:**
1. Hold the **Command (⌘)** key and press the **Spacebar**. A little search
   box appears in the middle of the screen. (Or click the magnifying-glass
   icon at the very top-right.)
2. Type the word:  `terminal`
3. Press **Return**. A small white or black window opens. That's the app
   you paste into.

### Step B — Paste the setup lines

Omar will send you a short block of setup lines. To use them:
1. Copy the block he sends (highlight it, then Ctrl+C on Windows or ⌘+C on
   Mac).
2. Click inside the command window from Step A.
3. Paste:  **Ctrl+V** on Windows, or **⌘+V** on Mac.
4. Press **Enter**. Let it run — it may take a few minutes and print a lot
   of text. That's normal.

If it asks you to install FFmpeg or Python first, Omar's block includes the
line for that too — just paste and press Enter when prompted.

### Step C — Start the Helper and connect it

1. Omar sends you two things: a **site address** and your **connect code**.
2. When the Helper starts, it asks for them. Paste the site address, press
   Enter, paste the connect code, press Enter.
3. Leave that little window open while you're making a video. Close it when
   you're done. (To make a video another day, just open the command app
   again and start the Helper the same way — it remembers your codes.)

Your videos are made right on your own computer and never live on anyone
else's.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
## PART 2 — Making videos (the easy part, every time)

1. Open the site link Omar sent. Sign in with your name and invite code
   (first time only — after that it remembers you).
2. First visit only: paste your DeepSeek API key. It's stored encrypted and
   never shown again. Get one at platform.deepseek.com (costs you a few
   cents per video).
3. Pick what you're making: Short, Long video, Commercial, Podcast, Course,
   Clips, or Custom.
4. Upload your footage. Big files are fine; if your internet hiccups, just
   try again and it picks up where it left off.
5. Make sure your Helper window (from Part 1) is open, then click
   **Make It**. Watch the progress.
6. Want changes? Type them in the chat, like "make the opening faster,"
   "bigger captions," or "remove the part about the dog." Anything that
   changes your words or the length shows you what it'll do first and waits
   for your OK.
7. When the checks pass, download your video. If a check fails it's marked
   **Needs Review** so you can watch it first — nothing broken is ever
   handed to you as finished.

That's everything. Nothing to keep running except your own computer while
you're actually making a video.
