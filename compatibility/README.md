# Compatibility corpus

Every published AutoEditor Law reader keeps its old archives here. CI runs the
current reader against the full corpus. Existing fixtures are append-only.

`2026-identity` tests the archive inventory, byte identity, schema lock, and
assurance-grade vocabulary. It is a contract fixture, not a decodable video or
a creative-quality claim. The v2 and v2.1 media incidents remain in
`tests/test_safety.py` and `docs/examples/` until their media is copied into a
fresh-reverification archive.
