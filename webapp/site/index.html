Step 1: connect DeepSeek

AutoEditor uses DeepSeek (an AI) to do the editing. You bring your own DeepSeek key so it's your account, not shared. DeepSeek charges your account based on usage. Paste your key below to unlock everything.

Don't have a key yet? Do this first (5 min)
  1. Open platform.deepseek.com and make a free account (email + password).
  2. Click Top up (or "Billing"). Add the smallest amount you are comfortable with after reading the current amount and price shown on DeepSeek's screen.
  3. Click API keys in the left menu, then Create new API key. Give it any name.
  4. It shows the key once. Click Copy. (It starts with sk-.)
  5. Come back here, paste it in the box above, and press Check & unlock.

Tip: keep that DeepSeek tab open until you've pasted the key here. You can't see it again later.

The moment your key works, a yellow Help button appears in the corner. That built-in assistant can walk you through anything from there on.

Your projects

    One-time: connect your computer

    Your videos are rendered on your own computer. The website uses private AutoEditor cloud storage to move your footage to the Helper and return the finished file. The Helper includes the editing engine, FFmpeg, speech models, HyperFrames, Remotion, fonts, and its own rendering browser. Deleting a project removes its uploaded and finished files from that storage.

    1. Download one installer:
      Windows PC Mac, Apple Silicon Mac, Intel Checking installers…
    2. Windows: open the downloaded .exe and press Yes if Windows asks whether to allow it. Mac: open the .dmg, drag AutoEditor Helper into Applications, then open it.
    3. Copy your Setup code below and paste it into the Helper.
    4. The Helper walks you through Pexels, Pixabay, ElevenLabs, and Remotion. You can connect each account or clearly skip it. A skipped source will not be available in your edits.
    5. Press Check My Setup and Start. Leave the Helper open while AutoEditor makes your video.
